Kube-VIM is a Kubernetes-native, ETSI NFV-MANO compliant Virtual Infrastructure
Manager. It presents the standard Or-Vi and Vi-Vnfm reference points to an NFV
orchestrator and realizes the requested resources as Kubernetes objects.
